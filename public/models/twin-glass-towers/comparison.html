Method and practical limits

Source-derived retopology

The source is aligned with a uniform ×100 scale and a +63.785398 Z translation. Candidate coordinates are already in meters. No per-view alignment is fitted to improve scores.

What the measurements cover

The silhouette pass temporarily uses opaque emission materials to isolate geometry. Red and blue regions reveal missing and extra projected coverage. Outline matching uses a two-pixel tolerance at the stated image resolution.

RGB error includes texture, normal, shading and geometric differences under this studio lighting. Local luminance SSIM uses nonoverlapping 8 × 8 blocks within the overlapping masks. Neither metric guarantees that every surface or detail is preserved.
